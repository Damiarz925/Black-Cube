## Changes and causes

- Tooltip exits previously set a universal 0.65-second close timer. There is now no linger timer. The stationary card touches the source item's side; pointer exit closes synchronously unless the pointer is inside the card. It stays open over the card, closes on leaving it, and replaces its contents on another item. Inventory scrolling invalidates the old card; a new item moving under the pointer may open its own current card. LateUpdate checks pointer geometry and source validity.
- Equipped slots use the same full Gear-driven card through a read-only hover component. The action reads EQUIPPED / CANNOT SCRAP and is disabled; the action callback and Inventory's existing equipped protection also reject dismantling. No unequip click/gesture was added. Closing the source panel or replacing equipped gear invalidates its card.
- `Gear.ApplyMods` previously assigned the raw WeaponBaseCrit roll directly to a probability field. Production tier 1 was 3–5, so a 3.47 roll became probability 3.47 and the tooltip multiplied by100 to show347%. The real attack then capped this to100%, making it an actual combat bug. Rolled percent points now convert once at ingestion (`3.47 / 100 = .0347`); starter weapons already use fractional `.05` and need no conversion. Existing production intrinsic tiers also scaled up to16–32% at tier5. Their ranges are now5–10% throughout, preserving the existing rolling machinery without adding a weapon-base variety system.
- The old formula applied weapon-local increased crit before adding other-gear flat base crit. With correct units, the requested example would previously yield `(5*2 + 2)*2 =24%`. Now ALL flat base points enter before local increased, followed by global increased: `(intrinsic + weapon flat + all other flat) * (1 + local increased sum) * (1 + global increased sum)`. Runtime values are fractions. Weapon-local increased is excluded from the global list. The capped final result is shared by the primary stats row and actual attack rolls. Enemy crit uses the same order for consistency.
- The primary display shows Critical Chance (Final) immediately below outgoing damage rows. Global increased crit is separately named Increased Critical Chance. The weapon card explicitly labels its own subtotal Weapon-only critical chance, distinct from the player's final total.
- Primary noncritical per-hit rows come directly from `BuildNonCriticalAttackContext().Hits`, only for actual nonzero components. Weapon base is labeled with its actual element. Pure Physical shows Physical per hit and Weapon Base Physical; existing mixed flat contributions show their individual types. Poison/Void retain their existing Physical modifier fallback but keep their actual outgoing labels. No new mixed damage system or hit/DOT arithmetic was introduced.
