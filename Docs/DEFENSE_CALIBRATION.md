# Defense Calibration

Open **Black-Cube → Balance Workbench → Open → Defense Calibration**.

The read-only resistance-pressure audit derives legal suffix families and tier midpoints from the production ModDatabase and item pools. Its rare-quality reference permits at most two suffixes per item and one copy of a family per item. “All Elemental Resistance” affects Fire, Cold and Lightning, never Void. The displayed suffix count is an investment-pressure example, **not** a reservation in the player gear optimizer. Earlier-level rows use the best legal tier available at that item level, capped at the 75% reference target. Re-run the audit after editing affix data.

For a survival check, select a Player Build and set the single **Combat Level**, trials, seed, duration and **No Renewable Life Recovery** flag, then choose **Run Full Level Sequence**. The simulator follows `WorldProgression.BossStage`: currently nine normal encounters and the boss at stage 10. Life and Mana carry between encounters; encounter-local Rage, buffs and enemy state reset. No-Recovery disables renewable player Life healing only; Mana recovery, damage, Armour, resistance and other normal combat logic remain active.

The result's **Run Context** records the exact Player Level, Combat Level, class/subclass/weapon, requested/completed trials, seed, recovery flag, build hash and production-data fingerprint. Changing controls or data afterward marks the old result **STALE**; it does not relabel the old figures. A cancelled run can have fewer completed trials than requested and must not be interpreted as a full sample. These are measurements of the supplied build, not automatically balanced targets for Life or Armour.

## Level-100 armour calibration (2026-09-27)

Combat level 100 resolves to Cinder Wastes, its tenth location, and 60% Corruption. A seeded production Enemy Gear Lab measurement generated 32 Rare builds for each of that location's eight normal-encounter archetypes (seeds 91000–91007). Weighting each archetype's mean noncritical basic hit by its encounter-table weight (8 for each of six ordinary archetypes, 2 for each of two elites) produced a **2,053.714** average hit after the armour-tier change. This is a Fire-region hit-size reference, treated hypothetically as Physical for the armour target; it is not a claim that those Fire hits are mitigated by Armour.

At level 100, four gear slots can each carry one perfect T1 flat-Armour and one perfect T1 increased-Armour roll; helmets and body armour can additionally carry a perfect T1 explicit Physical Damage Reduction roll. With T1 maxima of 1,305 flat Armour and 270% increased Armour, total Armour is `4 × 1305 × (1 + 4 × 2.70) = 61,596`. Two 0.10 explicit-reduction rolls add 20 percentage points. Against the reference hit, Armour provides 74.9953%, giving **94.9953% before the combat formula's 90% final clamp** (and no attacker penetration). This is an idealized Legendary-gear ceiling, not a realistic average build or a change to the global mitigation cap.

All five min/max tiers of both Armour affix families were multiplied by a common factor of approximately 4.028 and rounded to integer rolls, preserving their prior relative tier distribution within 1%. Evasion and explicit Physical Damage Reduction tiers were not changed. Stronger Armour can affect the enemy gear optimizer; the post-change sample above was rerun from the modified asset rather than relying solely on the initial 2,052.648 reference sample.
