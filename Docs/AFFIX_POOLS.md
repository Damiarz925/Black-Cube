Weapon base damage, base speed, and base crit are guaranteed base stats, not ordinary affixes. The Weapons row above is the union of all weapon elements; one weapon never rolls that entire union. All five elements share Prefixes `GenericDmg`, `PlusAllSkills` and Suffixes `GenericDotMult`, `BaseCritChance`, `CritChance`, `CritMult`, `AttackSpeed`, `ChanceToHitTwice`. Their extra eligible affixes are:

| Weapon element | Additional prefixes | Additional suffixes |

| Fire | FlatFire, FireDmg, FirePenetration, IgniteDmg | IgniteChance, IgniteDuration, IgniteTickRate |

Normal/Magic/Rare/Legendary explicit caps and per-side caps are defined by `AffixPolicy`. A listed stat can still require a minimum Item Level or be blocked by a duplicate/exclusive family; consult `ModDatabase.asset` and the Affix Analyzer for legal tiers and values.
