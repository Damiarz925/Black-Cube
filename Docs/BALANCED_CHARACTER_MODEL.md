# Balanced character model: current calibration workflow

The balanced character is assembled from historical enemy encounters, their actual seeded loot/currency budgets, legal production item rolls, bounded shared-currency crafting, a whole-inventory gear search, and a production-legal passive search. It does not reserve a fixed number of defensive affixes. The default Workbench comparison is **Ground Loot + Expected Crafting**; turn off **Use Expected Crafting** for Ground Loot Only. See `REALISTIC_PLAYER_MODEL.md` for exact steps and search bounds.

**Defense Calibration → Audit Resistance Tiers** reads the live ModDatabase, ordinary item pools, suffix policy, and tier gates. It reports the first item level at which each direct T1 Fire/Cold/Lightning/Void resistance is legal, the point when all are legal, their midpoint rolls, and a rare-quality reference investment toward 75% of each resistance. That investment obeys two suffixes per rare item and one copy of each family per item. All Elemental Resistance contributes to Fire/Cold/Lightning only. The same legal affix choices are evaluated with the best available earlier tier at each item level to produce a data-derived earlier progression reference. This is an investment-pressure estimate, not a prescribed gearset or final target schedule; a greedy legal cover can miss a combination that another legal search finds.

At this checkpoint the data audit reports direct T1 Fire/Cold/Lightning/Void options at item level **84**. Its six-suffix legal midpoint reference uses All Elemental choices available by item level **85** and then reaches 75% of all four. Reusing those exact affix-slot choices with earlier legal tiers yields approximately 30/30/30/29% at item level 20 and 70/70/70/75% at item level 60. These are conditional affix-investment ceilings, **not** predictions that historical level-20 or level-60 players own those exact items. Use the seeded inventory simulation to measure actual acquisition.

The whole-inventory optimizer computes a squared penalty from the **current** Life, Armour, Fire, Cold, Lightning, and Void deficits. Above a target, additional ordinary investment has zero direct deficit value, though it can still matter when a full-set substitution frees another slot. **Soft** permits an unmet floor and reports it; **Strict** requires every enabled final floor among tested sets. The joint gear/passive pass applies the same deficit score to legal passive choices and alternates with the full gearset search twice. These are bounded local searches, not guaranteed global optima.

**Defense Calibration → Run Full Level Sequence** simulates the actual nine normal encounters and boss, carrying Life and Mana forward. **No Renewable Life Recovery** disables Life regeneration and on-hit/on-kill healing only; it preserves base Life, Armour, resistances, offense, and Mana rules. Use it to measure full-level clear and boss reach/kill rates before editing any Life, Armour, recovery, or enemy curves. No final curves are authored by this pass. Reduced Shock/Chill Effect and explicit recovery targets are not yet included in the dynamic gear-deficit score; calibrate them separately before treating the model as final.
