# Step 12.5 ordinary-explicit affix baseline

Research snapshot: 2026-09-15. These are Path of Exile 1 ordinary random-item explicit modifiers used only as a numeric/item-class reference. Black-Cube keeps its own labels and mechanics. Influenced, Essence, Fossil, Veiled, Synthesis, Corrupted, Eldritch, crafted-only and unique rolls are excluded. The executable snapshot is `Assets/Scripts/PoedbAffixCatalog.cs`; this document records its provenance and deviations.

Primary source: [PoEDB ordinary modifiers list](https://poedb.tw/us/Modifiers_list). Cross-checks: [Cold damage](https://poedb.tw/us/Cold_damage), [Lightning damage](https://poedb.tw/us/Lightning_damage), [Fire resistance](https://poedb.tw/us/Fire_Resistance), and [PoEDB weapon-local damage explanation](https://poedb.tw/us/Weapon). Values below are weakest-to-strongest by item-level gate; the runtime gives the strongest applicable row T1. Higher item levels keep all lower eligible rows.

The slot column describes where **direct researched rows** replace Black-Cube values, not the entire rollable pool. Existing pooled AllRes on Helmet/Body Armour, Mana on Gloves/Helmet/Body Armour/Boots, and attributes on other Black-Cube slots retain their authored ladders rather than becoming silently unrollable. Fire/Cold/Lightning/Void ordinary resistance on Weapon remains deliberately ineligible; that case has no retained pooled weapon analogue. The seeded itemization audit checks that every still-pooled family has a reachable ladder.

Family selection weights remain separate from tier weights. For this foundation all eligible tiers within a family use equal temporary weighting. PoEDB spawn weights vary by exact weapon base class, which Black-Cube does not yet model; importing a sword/claw weight would be a false precision. Existing Black-Cube-only families retain their authored rolls and their current item-level gates, but use variable-length tier support and explicit Prefix/Suffix policy. They are not presented as researched PoE analogues and will be balanced in Step 13.
